This will be a space for examining investigative questions, exploring analytical methods, and explaining how technical findings can be communicated clearly.

## Questions worth exploring

<Disclosure title="Three questions behind an investigation">

- What question is an investigation trying to answer?
- Which observations support a finding?
- What remains unknown, and how should that uncertainty be explained?

</Disclosure>

## From an observation to an explanation

Future articles can walk through a method step by step, compare possible interpretations, or explore the reasoning behind an investigation diagram.

<Callout title="Make the reasoning visible">

An explanation should connect the question, the observations, and the interpretation so that readers can follow each step.

</Callout>

## Connecting ideas to casework

Where appropriate, articles can connect a broader topic with a case summary, allowing readers to explore both the method and its application.
