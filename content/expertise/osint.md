My investigative work includes researching publicly available information to examine digital footprints and identify leads that complement blockchain evidence. Earlier work in federal background investigations and military intelligence also involved gathering, evaluating, and reporting information from multiple sources.

I distinguish a lead from a supported finding and seek corroboration before drawing conclusions about the relationship between online activity and real-world people or organizations.
