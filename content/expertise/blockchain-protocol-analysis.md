A transaction must be understood in the context of the system that produced it. I examine blockchain structures and protocol behavior to interpret records, identify relevant patterns, and avoid treating superficially similar events as equivalent.

My experience and training include Ethereum, Cardano, Bitcoin, and other blockchain ecosystems. Analysis is scoped to the networks and mechanisms relevant to a particular matter.
