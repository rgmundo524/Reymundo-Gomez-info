title: Digital asset tracing
description_short: >-
  Reconstruct cryptocurrency movements and examine links between transactions, wallets, and
  reported incidents.
---

I trace digital asset movements to investigate where funds came from, where they moved, and how the activity relates to the facts of a matter. My experience includes cross-chain activity, wallet monitoring, and examining transaction patterns in fraud investigations.

The scope of a trace depends on the available data and the mechanisms of the networks and services involved. I do not assume that every transaction can be attributed to a person.
