I founded Go-Crypto to make professional blockchain analysis more accessible to individuals who are priced out of commercial investigative services. The initiative operates within Go Mobile Education Zone and combines investigation with education.

As lead investigator and a board member, I conduct fund tracing, wallet attribution analysis, forensic reporting, and ongoing wallet monitoring. My work combines blockchain intelligence platforms with internally developed analytical tools.

I also publish educational material and newsletters on scam prevention, wallet security, and common fraud patterns. Working with victims requires explaining investigative boundaries, evidentiary limitations, and realistic recovery pathways in plain language.
