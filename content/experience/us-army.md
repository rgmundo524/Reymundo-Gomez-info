I served in U.S. Army military intelligence, primarily within a future operations cell. My work combined data analysis and tactical analysis to assess potential threats and support operational planning.

I evaluated information from human intelligence, signals intelligence, geospatial intelligence, and open sources. Understanding the operational environment required considering political, social, and cultural factors alongside the available reporting.

The role also involved supervising teams, organizing research and case material, and advising senior leadership. This experience forms part of my background in structured analysis and communicating findings under demanding conditions.
