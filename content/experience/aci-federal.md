At ACI Federal, I interviewed subjects and sources to gather and verify information relevant to government security-clearance eligibility. I researched public records and obtained documentation from law enforcement, financial institutions, and educational institutions.

The role required assessing information from multiple sources, identifying issues that warranted further examination, and preparing comprehensive reports. I coordinated with fellow investigators, government officials, and adjudication professionals to support accurate investigative processes.
