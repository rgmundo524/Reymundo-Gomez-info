At CipherBlade, I investigate the movement of digital assets, examine unusual transaction patterns, and assess connections between on-chain activity and reported incidents. This work requires attention to the structure and behavior of each blockchain, including the protocols that affect how transactions should be interpreted.

I collaborate with law enforcement, cybersecurity specialists, legal counsel, and clients to develop investigative leads and explain findings. My work includes expert testimony on blockchain forensics, wallet security, and illicit on-chain activity.
