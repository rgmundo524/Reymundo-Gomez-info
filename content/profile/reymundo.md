I investigate cryptocurrency activity and explain what blockchain evidence can tell us. My work combines transaction tracing, an understanding of blockchain protocols, and custom analytical tools with clear reporting for attorneys, investigators, and clients.

My professional background spans commercial blockchain forensics, nonprofit fraud investigation and education, federal background investigations, and military intelligence. Across these settings, I have gathered evidence, assessed competing explanations, and communicated complex findings to people making consequential decisions.

In litigation work, I help legal teams understand digital asset movements and the technical context behind them. My experience includes forensic reports, visual exhibits, and expert witness work involving cryptocurrency disputes. I also develop analytical workflows when existing platforms do not provide the coverage an investigation needs.
