IC3's FAQ explains the complaint process and which supporting records to keep. Preserve original documents and communications securely, and save a copy of your completed complaint.

The FAQ also explains that IC3 does not provide investigation status updates. Decisions about investigating reports are made by the relevant law enforcement agencies.
