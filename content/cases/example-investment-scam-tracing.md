## Approach

Use supplied transaction identifiers to reconstruct fund movements and identify relevant services or additional investigative leads. Keep on-chain observations separate from claims made in communications.

## Deliverable

A fund-flow diagram and transaction schedule explaining observed transfers and limits to further tracing.
