## Approach

Compare supplied account records with transaction history for identified wallets. Organize transfers by date and asset, and distinguish observed activity from assumptions about ownership.

## Deliverable

A transaction timeline and a written explanation of the evidence, unresolved ownership questions, and records that would help complete the review.
