I am building this site to bring together my professional background, investigation
experience, personal interests, and public projects. Each subject lives in a
Markdown file so I can reuse and update the content without editing page layouts.

The site uses Astro, amCharts, Pagefind, Astro Icon, and tsParticles, with a local
development environment managed through devenv.
